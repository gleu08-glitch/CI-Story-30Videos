## Cross-Platform Strategy

### YouTube Shorts
- **Audience:** Developers, students, tech enthusiasts
- **Goal:** Evergreen educational content, searchability
- **Format:** 45-60 seconds, clear visuals, good lighting
- **CTA:** Subscribe, watch full series
- **Tags:** GitHub, CI/CD, DevOps, automation

### TikTok
- **Audience:** Gen Z, younger developers, curious tech learners
- **Goal:** Viral reach, trending sounds, engagement
- **Format:** 30-45 seconds, fast-paced, hook first
- **CTA:** Follow, like, comment, share
- **Sounds:** Trending audio when applicable

---

## Optimization Notes

- **Hook timing:** First 3 seconds must grab attention
- **Text overlay:** Keep subtitles large and readable
- **Pacing:** Vary speed to maintain interest
- **Consistency:** Keep same intro/outro across series
- **Branding:** Use consistent thumbnails, logos, colors
